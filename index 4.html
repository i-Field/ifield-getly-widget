FIELD NOTES — STUDIO

Nature observed, technology applied.

iField is an independent digital studio documenting wild behavior on the edge of a countryside homestead — squirrels, redstarts, hedgehogs — in original 4K footage, photography and ambient sound.

Original work. Independent creation.

LOCATION: rural homestead, edge of forest FORMAT: 4K · HEVC · 60fps SOUND: original ambient, unedited STATUS: active field documentation

iField explores the intersection of nature, science, technology and digital art. We create and curate original photography, 4K nature footage, atmospheric sounds and experimental digital products inspired by the natural world.

Our goal is simple: to preserve moments, explore ideas, and turn real observations into unique digital experiences — for filmmakers, documentarians, educators and anyone building something that needs to feel real.
