🌿 iField Getly Widget — Live Demo

This page demonstrates the floating "Support iField" widget. Look at the bottom-right corner — click the pill to open the panel.

To embed on your own page, copy widget.css, widget.js and config.js next to your HTML file, then add before </body>:

<link rel="stylesheet" href="widget.css">
<script src="config.js"></script>
<script src="widget.js"></script>
